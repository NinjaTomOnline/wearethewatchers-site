CUSTOS Shipping

Draft policy: CUSTOS has not opened checkout yet. This page sets the policy shape, but final shipping times, rates, countries, carriers, and tracking rules depend on the selected storefront and POD provider.

Current launch status

  • No products are currently available for purchase.
  • No shipping rates or delivery promises are published yet.
  • Provider selection and sample approval are required before shipping terms become final.

Launch shipping policy target

  • Made-to-order products will enter production after purchase.
  • Tracking should be sent automatically by the storefront or fulfillment provider when available.
  • Multiple-item orders may ship separately if fulfilled by different provider facilities.
  • International shipping, customs, duties, VAT, and local import fees must be disclosed before global checkout is enabled.

Pre-launch checklist

  • Pick POD provider and blank products.
  • Confirm production windows per product.
  • Confirm shipping countries, rates, tracking, and support workflow.
  • Run a sample order and verify packaging/tracking email copy.
